Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This application is for personal use by its owner only. This policy explains how it handles information.

Information accessed

The application accesses only the information its owner authorizes through the sign-in and consent screen, and only the minimum needed for the features its owner uses.

How information is used

Information is used solely to provide the application's features to its owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Information is stored on systems controlled by the owner and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Access credentials are kept private and are never published.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not transferred to anyone else, except where required by law.

Google user data

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept only as long as needed for the application's features. Access can be revoked at any time from the account's security settings (for Google accounts, at myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
